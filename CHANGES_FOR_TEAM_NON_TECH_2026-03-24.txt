ECS UPDATE SUMMARY (NON-TECHNICAL)
Date: 24 Mar 2026

This is a business-friendly summary of all major updates currently worked on (backend + frontend), so you can share with operations, sales, support, and leadership.

--------------------------------------------------
BIG PICTURE
--------------------------------------------------

- We improved both the receipt experience and supporting internal workflows.
- Customer-facing receipt format is now clearer and more professional.
- Internal data flow is being standardized so reports and exports are more reliable.
- Frontend screens used by staff (receipt flow, transaction view, dashboards) are also being actively improved.

--------------------------------------------------
CUSTOMER-FACING RECEIPT IMPROVEMENTS
--------------------------------------------------

1) Receipt PDF now has a cleaner design
- Information is organized into clear boxed sections.
- The page is easier to scan and verify quickly.
- Visual format now feels more professional and standardized.

2) Same receipt style now works across all key product types
- Fixed Deposit / Government FD
- Mutual Funds (SIP, STP, SWP, Switch Over)
- Insurance
- Bond/NCD
- Misc services

3) Investment and payment details are easier to read
- Details now appear in aligned blocks instead of long line-by-line text.
- This helps branch staff and customers validate details faster.

4) Branding/header adjustments completed
- Removed extra company name line in header as requested.
- Logo size increased for better visibility.

5) Reduced unnecessary extra-page output
- Receipt spacing and layout were refined to avoid accidental overflow.

Important note:
- Financial values and business data shown in receipts remain the same.
- Main change is readability, consistency, and presentation quality.

--------------------------------------------------
BACKEND BUSINESS IMPACT (UNDER THE HOOD)
--------------------------------------------------

1) Better consistency in receipt data
- Backend receipt handling is being aligned to one consistent structure.
- Transaction details are being standardized so different product flows behave more uniformly.

2) Data migration support added/updated
- Migration scripts were updated/added to align older records with the newer structure.
- This reduces future reporting mismatches and maintenance issues.

3) Branch/export/report alignment improvements
- Related backend routes were updated to better match the new receipt and transaction structure.
- Expected outcome: cleaner downstream reporting and export reliability.

--------------------------------------------------
FRONTEND WORK IN PROGRESS (STAFF-FACING SCREENS)
--------------------------------------------------

The following areas are actively updated and not fully finalized yet:

1) Receipt creation and final review flow
- Multi-step receipt flow screens are being refined.
- Product-specific steps (FD, Insurance, Bond/NCD, transaction step) are being aligned.

2) Receipt view/print experience
- Receipt display and related components are being adjusted to stay consistent with backend improvements.

3) Dashboards and transaction pages
- Branch dashboard, main dashboard, and transactions page have active improvements.

4) Data normalization in UI
- Frontend data mapping is being aligned with backend structure for more consistent display and fewer edge-case issues.

--------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS MEANS FOR BRANCH TEAMS
--------------------------------------------------

- Faster manual verification of receipts.
- More consistent look across different investment products.
- Lower confusion around transaction/payment fields.
- Better confidence that saved data, displayed data, and export/report data stay aligned.

--------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO COMMUNICATE TO LEADERSHIP
--------------------------------------------------

- This release wave is focused on quality and consistency, not just visual changes.
- Customer documents now look more premium and standardized.
- Internal data standardization work reduces long-term errors and support overhead.
- Some frontend improvements are still in active progress and will be finalized in follow-up commits.
