# What’s New – Update for Everyone

This note is for staff and managers (non-technical). It describes recent improvements in plain language.

---

## SIP receipts: “Daily” frequency option

**What changed**

When you create a **SIP (Systematic Investment Plan)** receipt, you can now choose **Daily** as the investment frequency.

**Before:** You could only choose Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annual.

**Now:** You can also choose **Daily** if the product supports daily SIP.

**What you need to do**

- Nothing. The new option appears in the same “Frequency” dropdown when you create a SIP receipt. Pick “Daily” when it applies.
